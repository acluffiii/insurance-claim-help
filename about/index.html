About this site

Who writes Insurance Claim Help, and how to read it

Insurance Claim Help is a plain-English guide site about auto and home insurance claims. It starts with hail damage and has a Texas focus, because that is where a lot of the confusion — and a lot of the storms — show up.

The project is written by Claim Mogul and is connected to DentDOG PDR, a paintless dent repair shop in the Houston area. There is a sister site, After the Hail, about hail repair and PDR for Texas car owners. This site is about understanding claims, not about selling a repair.

Educational only

Nothing here is legal advice, insurance advice, or a claim-handling service. A guide can explain how coverage is usually described in official consumer materials. It cannot tell you what your policy says, what your insurer will pay, or what you should do in a dispute. Read your policy, including the declarations page, and talk with your insurer or a licensed professional about your own situation.

For official Texas consumer help, use the Texas Department of Insurance, including its pages on getting a claim paid and filing a complaint.

How sources work

Guides cite official consumer sources that were actually opened and checked, such as the Texas Department of Insurance, the Insurance Information Institute, and the National Association of Insurance Commissioners. Community posts from X or Reddit may be mentioned when they show what people are confused about. Those posts are not proof of how any company handles claims, and this site does not invent quotations or statistics.

How a new guide gets added

New pages are written from a public topic queue, one at a time, and logged in a changelog kept with the project files. Each guide lists the sources it used.

Educational content only. This site is not legal advice, insurance advice, or a promise that any policy will pay a claim. Coverage, deductibles, and claim decisions depend on your policy, your insurer, and the facts of your loss. Read your policy and talk with your insurer or a licensed professional about your situation. For official Texas consumer help, start at the Texas Department of Insurance.
